About the institution

Thirty years in Rustam.

Introduction

Since its establishment in 1996, Sudhum Children Academy has never looked back. The institution has held top distinction status in the S.S.C examinations conducted by BISE Mardan since 2005.

The academy extended itself by setting up Sudhum Science College in 2006, and established a Sudhum Girls Academy the very next year — to spread the light to the most oppressed class of our rural society.

The boys' campus is located at Shereen Abad, Rustam, on the Pir Baba Road, a short distance from Rustam main bazaar. The girls' campus sits half way between the main bazaar and the boys' campus. The primary block forms the backbone of the institution's struggle to kindle and enlighten the hearts and minds of its children.

At present the institution is exclusively meant for science studies, but keeping in view the utility of humanities, the administration is also considering the commencement of humanities classes in the near future.

Academics

Four sections, one standard.

Teaching is organised into four sections, each with its own faculty. The syllabus follows the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Textbook Board, with S.S.C and H.S.S.C examinations conducted by BISE Mardan.

Section 01 · Nursery to Kachi

Pre-Primary

Early literacy, numeracy, Nazira Qur'an and spoken English. Eleven teachers are assigned to this section alone.

Section 02 · Classes 1–5

Primary

The block the institution calls the backbone of its work. Twenty-five teachers, including Qaris and B.Ed holders.

Section 03 · Classes 6–8

Middle

Subject teaching begins. Specialists for mathematics, science, Islamiyat, computing and languages.

Section 04 · Classes 9–10

Section 05 · Classes 11–12

How admission works.

Prospectus and admission form can be obtained from the college office after the declaration of annual results. You can also start the application online and bring the printed slip with you.

Admission rules

Nine rules.

  1. Application for admission must be submitted on the original printed form attached to the prospectus. A photocopy of the admission form is not accepted.
  2. The application, complete in all respects, must be submitted to the college office on or before the notified date during office hours.
  3. The merit list is displayed on the notice board of the college within 24 hours of the test.
  4. Short-listed applicants must register themselves in the college office and take a date for interview.
  5. Applicants must appear for the interview with their parents or guardian on the specified date. Those who fail to appear are not considered for admission.
  6. To confirm admission, successful applicants must make all required payments within two working days of the interview, failing which the admission is treated as cancelled.
  7. Applicants must make sure their course selection is correct. Faculty or subjects cannot be changed two weeks after classes commence.
  8. Admission granted to a student of any class or section is final.
  9. Successful applicants and their parents or legal guardian give a written undertaking that any breach of the disciplinary rules will lead to expulsion without prior warning or notice.

Eligibility

Who can apply.

  1. Students with an E grade or compartments in the S.S.C examination are not eligible for admission to H.S.S.C.
  2. Candidates who passed the S.S.C annual examination in the current year or the year before are eligible.
  3. Candidates found guilty of moral turpitude, or convicted of a criminal offence by a court of law, are not eligible.
  4. Admission remains at the sole discretion of the Principal, who reserves the right to refuse admission to any candidate without assigning a reason.

How aggregate marks are calculated

A × 0.70  +  B × 0.30  =  C

  • A — entry test marks
  • B — S.S.C or equivalent marks
  • C — aggregate marks used for the merit list

Fees

Payment rules.

All students pay the admission fee and other dues at the time of admission, in accordance with the laid down policy. After admission, tuition fee and other dues are charged monthly or bimonthly across twelve months.

  • Admission fee and four months' tuition fee are submitted in advance at the time of admission.
  • Tuition fee is submitted at the end of every month, and before the beginning of a new academic year.
  • Promotion fee is deposited along with the tuition fee, in advance.
  • All fees are submitted at the accounts office.
  • Special fee relief is given to siblings studying at the institute.

Parents are requested to discuss fee and admission dues with the accountant at the accounts office, rather than taking the matter to the Principal's office.
